Django E-Commerce with AI-Driven Sales Automation

A full-featured Django e-commerce platform enabling guest browsing, mandatory secure checkout, instant WhatsApp inquiry, an AI chatbot for buyer assistance, and automated email order confirmations—streamlining sales from discovery to delivery.

About The Project

Modern retail success hinges on removing friction at every touchpoint—from product discovery to post-purchase fulfillment. To address this, I engineered a comprehensive Django-based e-commerce ecosystem that optimizes both the consumer experience and administrative workflow. The platform allows unauthenticated users to freely browse an extensive catalog (indoor/outdoor plants, succulents, and accessories) with advanced search filters, lowering the barrier to entry. However, to ensure transactional security and data integrity, mandatory user authentication is enforced at checkout—protecting both the business and its customers. To bridge offline and digital sales, I integrated a persistent WhatsApp inquiry button, enabling instant seller-buyer communication. Furthermore, I deployed an intelligent AI chatbot to autonomously handle pre-sales queries, reducing support overhead and improving response times. Firebase serves as a supplementary real-time data layer alongside Django's ORM, while a robust admin panel gives staff full CRUD control over inventory and pricing. Critically, I implemented an automated email microservice that instantly dispatches detailed order summaries to administrators, ensuring swift fulfillment and zero oversight gaps.
