Drone-Based Autonomous PPE Compliance & Safety System

An AI-driven drone surveillance system using YOLOv11 to detect industrial safety helmet violations in real-time. It automates aerial site inspections, instantly alerts supervisors, and enforces workplace safety compliance without human intervention.

About The Project

Workplace safety is a critical liability for industrial sectors, yet traditional manual monitoring is costly, intermittent, and riddled with human error. To solve this, I engineered an autonomous safety ecosystem that bridges the gap between aerial robotics and deep learning. I integrated a custom-trained YOLOv11 object detection model with a live drone camera feed to patrol construction sites and manufacturing plants. The system processes the video stream in real-time, accurately classifying workers based on helmet compliance despite varying altitudes, lighting, and angles. Upon detecting a violation, the architecture instantly triggers a multi-tier alert system—capturing timestamped evidence and pushing immediate notifications to command-center dashboards and site supervisors' mobile devices. This project demonstrates my proficiency in deploying Computer Vision models beyond the "Jupyter Notebook" phase, handling low-latency inference, and developing robust event-driven architectures. By automating safety enforcement, this solution reduces inspection costs, ensures 24/7 regulatory compliance (OSHA standards), and most importantly, protects human lives by preventing fatal head injuries at their source.
