Super start worldwide Limousine

A fully responsive Django-powered booking ecosystem for a US-based luxury limo service. It streamlines reservations via dynamic quotes, secure document verification, and multi-step booking forms, replacing manual phone/email chaos with a seamless digital client journey.

About The Project

In the luxury transportation industry, client expectations are high, and booking friction costs revenue. I developed a comprehensive web solution for SuperStar Worldwide Limousine to digitize and automate their entire client-acquisition funnel. Built on Django's robust MVC framework, this platform integrates three core operational pillars: an intelligent instant-quote generator that calculates trip costs based on vehicle type, duration, and stops; a detailed reservation system with multi-address mapping; and a high-security document upload portal (Verified by VikingCloud) for billing and verification—reducing administrative errors and fraud risk. I engineered custom database models to handle complex relational data (clients, trips, vehicles) and implemented secure form validation to protect sensitive user data. The front-end is optimized for both desktop and mobile, ensuring a luxury feel that matches the brand. By shifting their operations online, I helped the client reduce manual booking time by an estimated 60%, enabling their dispatch team to focus on service quality rather than paperwork.
